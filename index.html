OUR VISION

Science for the soil. Intelligence for the farmer.

AI Crop Disease Detector is imagined as a farmer-first digital platform: mobile access, soil-test-based crop planning, climate and water-aware suggestions, crop-health monitoring, responsible bio-input guidance, and market comparison across Telangana and India. The long-term goal is to make trusted agricultural information easier to access wherever a farmer works.
